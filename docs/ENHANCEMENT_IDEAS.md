# Suggested next improvements

These are proposals, not shipped features. Go Dance and steady-view controls shipped in 1.2.0. Version 1.3.0 adds twelve-pose running cycles, larger textures and interpolated presentation. Version 1.4.0 adds ducking hazards and continuously articulated Go Dance movement.

1. **Physical-device motion review and pose refinement.** Play the twelve-pose loops at actual phone and tablet sizes. Refine near/far leg separation and foot-contact timing with an animator if the generated poses still read as sliding. Browser frame timing does not establish individual comfort or mobile performance.
2. **Tea-earned outfits.** Let players spend collected tea on original outfit colors, ghutra patterns and dance accessories. Keep rewards cosmetic so obstacle timing stays fair.
3. **Short daily routes.** A reproducible 45–60 second course with one clear challenge: collect a tea trail, finish without a hit, or land three precise jumps. Offline seeds can keep it accessible without accounts.
4. **More dance settings.** Empty courtyard, beach evening, and wedding hall backgrounds, all stationary. Add coordinated group arrangements; individual clapping, shuffling and disco steps are now available. Traditional group steps would benefit from a cultural review.
5. **A readable obstacle language.** Give moving hazards a consistent anticipation pose and short sound cue. Introduce each new hazard in a safe stretch before mixing it into harder patterns.
6. **Party keepsakes.** A locally exported screenshot and named saved arrangements, so players can return to a favorite scene. Native sharing needs a deliberate platform implementation and should not upload anything automatically.

Priority: motion comfort and physical-device playtests first, then cosmetic rewards and short repeatable challenges. No store approval or physical-device performance is inferred from browser tests.

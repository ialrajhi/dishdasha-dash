## App name

Dishdasha Dash

## Short description

Jump, duck and collect tea across Kuwait. Build your own party in Go Dance.

## Full description

A little Kuwaiti adventure, one tea glass at a time.

Run from the neighborhood to the wedding in Dishdasha Dash. Time your jumps over street obstacles, hold Duck beneath hanging awnings, and look out for the golden dallah hidden in every stage.

EXPLORE FIVE STAGES
Travel through the neighborhood, the co-op, Al-Mubarakiya, the desert and the wedding. Illustrated streets, Arabic shop signs and Kuwait’s landmarks give each stop its own character.

LEARN TWO MOVES
Try the short jump-and-duck practice before your first run. Tap for a small hop, hold longer to jump higher, and stay low under awnings. Replay the lesson from How to play whenever you want.

MAKE YOUR OWN PARTY
Take a break in Go Dance. Choose from 12 characters, place and move them around the floor, try 11 dance moves and switch between six music tracks. Save your party on your device and return to it later.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Use touch controls or a compatible controller. English menus are the default, Arabic menus are available, and the game’s streets and signs remain Arabic. Adjust music and effects, reduce visual effects, or keep the skyline still with Steady view.

Your progress, scores, settings and dance party are saved on your device. The installed game is designed to work offline, with no game account, advertising or in-app purchases.

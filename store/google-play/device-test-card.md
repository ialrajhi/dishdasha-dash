# Dishdasha Dash — Android tester card

Candidate: 1.5.0 · package com.falrajhi.dishdashadash

Record device model, Android version, build version and date. Test on at least one smaller Android phone and one tablet; include a modest-performance device. Browser viewport checks do not replace these tests.

1. **Fresh install:** reach the menu, start practice, deliberately miss the box, complete both moves. Confirm no hearts or scores are lost in practice. Skip and replay it from How to play.
2. **All five stages:** finish each with jump/duck controls, retry a failed run, collect a golden dallah, and check the next stage unlocks. Note any confusing or unavoidable obstacle.
3. **Interruptions:** pause during a jump and beneath an awning. Try Home, app switch, lock/unlock and return. The run must stay paused; no invisible movement, loss of health, stuck inputs or background music.
4. **Offline:** after installing, turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data. Force-stop and reopen. Check menu, practice, every stage, all 12 dancers, six tracks and software/font licenses.
5. **Saves:** change sound settings, finish a stage and arrange a party. Force-stop/reopen, then reboot the device. Confirm settings, progress and party return. Check update from the preceding test build without uninstalling.
6. **Screen and input:** landscape in both directions, cutouts/system bars, tablet window resizing, rapid jump/duck changes, two-finger holds and any controller claimed in the listing. No clipped controls or accidental page scrolling.
7. **Go Dance:** add, move, clone and remove dancers; switch all 11 moves and six tracks; undo; pause; leave and return. Check the coffee pot’s handle and character joints for visual artifacts.
8. **Comfort:** play for 20 minutes. Note stutter, heat, battery impact, dizziness, illegible text or tiring controls. Compare Steady view and Reduce effects. Check quiet/loud effects through the device’s speakers and headphones.
9. **Reset:** confirm Cancel preserves progress. Confirm Reset clears progress and party while retaining settings, and offers practice again.
10. **Play candidate:** repeat install, upgrade and launch through Play internal testing using the upload-signed bundle. Review Google’s actual pre-launch report.

Report each issue with the step, expected behavior, actual behavior, device/build, and a screenshot or short recording when useful. Do not include account passwords or unrelated personal information.
